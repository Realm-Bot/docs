# Getting Started

(insert image of setup process here)

Follow these steps to set up Realm Bot for your server.

## 1. Invite the Bot
Use the `/invite` command or [click here](https://realmbot.dev/invite) to invite Realm Bot to your Discord server.
Ensure you grant the required permissions (Administrator is recommended for full functionality).

## 2. Link Your Account
Realms are owned by Xbox Live accounts. You must link your Xbox account to Realm Bot so it can access your Realms.
1. Go to the [Dashboard](https://dashboard.realmbot.dev).
2. Login with Discord.
3. Link your Microsoft/Xbox account.

## 3. Connect to Discord
Once your account is linked on the dashboard, run the connect command in your Discord server:
- **Command**: `/connect`
- **Action**: This will verify your linked account and connect the current Discord server to your Xbox profile.
- **Result**: You should see a success message listing your available Realms.

## 4. Configuration
You can now use the `/realm` commands to manage your Realm!
- View commands: `/help`
- Configure settings: [Web Dashboard](https://dashboard.realmbot.dev)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If you encounter issues connecting, ensure your Xbox Privacy Settings allow external apps/bots.
