# `/warn add`

Issue a warning to a player. If the warning points exceed the threshold, the player may be automatically banned.

- **Permissions**: Authorized Users Only
- **Usage**: `/warn add <user> <reason> [realm]`

**Arguments**:
- `user` (Required): The player to warn.
- `reason` (Required): The reason for the warning. Must match a configured reason.
- `realm` (Optional): The realm to warn on. Defaults to all.
