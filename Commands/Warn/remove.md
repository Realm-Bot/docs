# `/warn remove`

Remove a warning from a player.

- **Permissions**: Authorized Users Only
- **Usage**: `/warn remove <user> <reason> [realm]`

**Arguments**:
- `user` (Required): The player to remove warning from.
- `reason` (Required): The original warning reason to remove.
