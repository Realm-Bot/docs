# `/connect`

Connect your Xbox account to the discord server. This is a **required step** for using most Realm Bot features.

- **Permissions**: Authorized Users Only
- **Usage**: `/connect`
- **Response**: Checks if your Xbox account is linked to your Realm Bot dashboard account. If linked, it connects the Discord server to your account, giving the bot access to your Realms.
