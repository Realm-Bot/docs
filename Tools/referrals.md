# Referrals

Open your dashboard **account menu > Referrals** to share Realm Bot with another Realm owner. Sign in with Discord and link your Xbox account to generate your personal link and code.

## Make a Referral

1. Share your link before your friend connects their Realm. Alternatively, they can enter your code in the optional `referral_code` field of [`/connect`](../Commands/Utility/connect.md).
2. Your friend must complete a [server connection](../setup.md) with an initialised, non-expired Bedrock Realm within the **30-day attribution window**. Clicking the link or making a purchase does not qualify them.
3. Return to **Referrals** to see reward progress, days earned and entries marked pending, completed, expired or revoked.

## Eligibility and Rewards

The friend's Xbox account must not already be connected to a server. Connection or activity in the previous **90 days** can disqualify a returning owner. Self-referrals and reuse of a Discord account, Xbox account or Realm that has already earned a reward do not qualify.

| Recipient | Realm Bot Premium reward |
|---|---|
| Referrer | Normally **30 days** per qualifying referral, up to the default cap of **five** rewarded referrals. |
| Eligible friend | **14 days**, even if the referrer has already reached the cap. |

Reward time extends an existing later Premium end date rather than replacing it. If an entry is pending, check whether the friend completed the connection with a qualifying Realm within the window. Rewards can be revoked for abuse.
