# Realm Bot Premium

Realm Bot offers a premium subscription service that unlocks additional features and benefits for users. By subscribing to Realm Bot premium, you can access advanced moderation tools, priority support, and more. This page provides an overview of the premium subscription service and how you can upgrade to premium.

## What is Realm Bot Premium?

Realm Bot premium is a subscription service that offers additional features and benefits to users of the bot. By upgrading to premium, you can access advanced moderation tools, priority support, and other exclusive features that enhance your experience with Realm Bot.

## How to Get Realm Bot Premium

To upgrade to Realm Bot premium, go to the [Realm Bot website](https://www.realmbot.dev/premium) and click the "Upgrade to Premium" button. Follow the instructions to complete the subscription process and unlock premium features for your server.
