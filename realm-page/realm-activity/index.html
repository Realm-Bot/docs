# Realm Activity

By
Frazer

The Realm Activity page provides a structured view of how your Realm is being played over time. It is designed to help owners and staff understand:

  • Who is active (and when)
  • How often players are joining (sessions)
  • How long players are spending in the Realm (playtime)
  • When your Realm is most active (peak hours)
  • Recent activity for operational context (recent sessions)

This page is particularly useful for:

  • Staffing coverage and moderation planning
  • Event scheduling and announcement timing
  • Verifying whether activity is increasing or dropping
  • Identifying unusual usage patterns

# Overview and Time Range

Realm Activity is calculated over a selected timeframe (for example, Last 7 days). Adjusting the timeframe updates the charts and totals to reflect that period.

Key summary metrics shown at the top typically include:

  • Total Sessions - total number of recorded play sessions
  • Total Playtime - cumulative playtime across all sessions
  • Active Players - number of unique players active in the selected period
  • Average Session - average session duration during the selected period

Note: These metrics reflect the selected timeframe. If you switch from “Last 7 days” to a larger range, totals and averages will change accordingly.

# Player Search and Filtering

Use the search bar to locate players and quickly review activity for a specific account. This is useful when verifying:

  • whether a player has been online recently
  • session frequency
  • patterns that may correlate with incidents or reports

# Daily Activity

The Daily Activity chart provides a day-by-day overview across the selected timeframe. It typically visualises:

  • Sessions - how many sessions occurred per day
  • Unique Players - how many distinct players were active per day

This view helps you identify:

  • consistent activity vs. spikes
  • days where the Realm was inactive
  • growth patterns before/after events or updates

# Peak Hours

The Peak Hours chart shows the 24-hour distribution of activity. It is designed to answer the question:

“At what times is my Realm most active?”

This is particularly valuable for:

  • scheduling staff coverage for peak windows
  • timing announcements for maximum reach
  • planning events during high participation periods

Operational Tip: If you notice repeated activity spikes at a consistent hour block, consider scheduling maintenance or updates outside those windows to minimise disruption.

# Recent Sessions

The Recent Sessions table provides a quick audit-friendly list of the latest play sessions, typically including:

  • Player - the account that played
  • Duration - how long the session lasted
  • Started - how recently the session began (relative time)
  • Date - the exact timestamp for record accuracy

This section is useful for:

  • confirming whether your Relay Account has been online recently
  • checking whether a player was active during a specific incident window
  • providing staff with immediate operational context

# Interpreting the Data

When reviewing activity, keep the following in mind:

  • Short sessions may indicate quick join-checks, instability, or connection issues.
  • Low unique players with high sessions may indicate a small group playing frequently.
  • High peak-hour concentration may suggest a strong time-zone bias in your community.
  • Sudden spikes can align with public code exposure, events, raids, or content updates.

# Troubleshooting Notes

If the page appears empty or incomplete:

  • Confirm you are viewing the correct Realm.
  • Ensure there has been activity within the selected timeframe.
  • If your activity system relies on an in-game relay/account presence in your deployment, verify it is operating correctly.
  • Try expanding the timeframe to confirm whether older activity exists.
