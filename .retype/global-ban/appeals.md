# Appeals

An appeal asks Realm Hub reviewers to reconsider an eligible Global Ban record. Appeals are available only to the linked Microsoft/Xbox account that owns the affected Realm Hub record.

## Eligibility

To appeal, all of the following must be true:

- The record belongs to the Realm Hub Database.
- The record is eligible for appeal.
- You are signed in to Realm Bot with Discord.
- The affected Microsoft/Xbox account is linked to your Realm Bot account.
- No other active appeal exists for the same case.

ARAS / Realms+ and custom GitHub list records cannot be appealed through Realm Hub.

## Submit an Appeal

1. Sign in to the Realm Bot dashboard with Discord.
2. Link the Microsoft/Xbox account affected by the ban.
3. Open the public Realm Hub record and select **Submit an Appeal**, or visit `/appeal`.
4. Confirm that the displayed Xbox profile and case are correct.
5. Explain why the record should be reconsidered.
6. Optionally attach up to three supported evidence images.
7. Confirm that the appeal is accurate.
8. Complete the security check.
9. Submit the appeal.

Appeal explanations must be between **10 and 4,000 characters**.

## Appeal Evidence

Evidence is optional, but any attached files use the same limits as report evidence:

| Requirement | Limit |
|---|---|
| Supported formats | PNG, JPG, JPEG, WebP, and GIF |
| Maximum size per file | 10 MB |
| Maximum combined attachment size | 20 MB |
| Maximum attachments | 3 |

Provide material that directly supports the reason for reconsideration. Do not include unrelated personal information, credentials, or evidence you are not entitled to share.

## While an Appeal Is Active

Only one active appeal can exist for a case at a time. A **Submitted** or **Under Review** appeal can be withdrawn from **Your Appeals**.

> **Important:** Submitting an appeal does not suspend or remove the ban. The record remains active until an accepted appeal revokes it.

## Appeal Outcomes

### Accepted

The Realm Hub record is revoked. Connected Realm protections are reconciled asynchronously, so native Realm ban changes may not be immediate.

### Denied

The Realm Hub record remains active and enforcement continues.

### Withdrawn

The active appeal is closed without changing the Realm Hub record.

## Troubleshooting

### No eligible record appears

- Confirm the affected Microsoft/Xbox account is linked.
- Confirm the public record belongs to that exact XUID.
- Confirm the record is a native Realm Hub record.
- Check whether another active appeal already exists.

### The wrong Xbox profile appears

Do not submit the appeal. Return to account linking and select the Microsoft/Xbox account that owns the affected record.

### Evidence is rejected

- Confirm the file is PNG, JPG, JPEG, WebP, or GIF.
- Keep each file at or below 10 MB.
- Keep the combined total at or below 20 MB.
- Attach no more than three files.

### The security check fails

Refresh the page, complete a new security check, and submit again.

### An accepted appeal has not removed a Realm ban

- Allow time for asynchronous reconciliation.
- Ask the Realm owner to use **Check Global Ban Database** and **Check Realm**.
- Verify the record is shown as revoked.
- Confirm the Realm's native banlist separately.

### An external record is incorrect

Contact the provider that owns the record. Realm Hub cannot process appeals for ARAS / Realms+ or a custom GitHub list.
